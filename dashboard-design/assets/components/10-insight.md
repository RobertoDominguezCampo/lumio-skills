# Insight card

**Question it answers:** What should we do, and why?

## When to use it
There is one clear action with a cost or benefit.

## When not to
There is no action yet: don't fake one.

## Anatomy
- Small label: what to do
- The action in one sentence
- A small chart that proves it, the part to act on marked
- The maths in one line
- The impact as a big number + what it means

## Size
512×440. Only sizes from the layout grid.

## Style rules
- Follow the tokens and the quality bar in README.md.
- Title 19 px bold, hero 30 px bold, labels 13 px, axis 11 px. Tabular numbers.
- One accent highlight only. Red only for bad values, green only for on track.
- No empty space: if it doesn't fill the card, use the smaller size.

## In Power BI
Power BI: shape with accent fill + text box + small bar chart; numbers from measures.
