# Donut + breakdown

**Question it answers:** What is the mix?

## When to use it
2 to 5 parts of one whole, when one part is the story.

## When not to
More than 5 parts or small differences: use bars.

## Anatomy
- Insight title with the key share in accent
- Donut: the key part in accent, the rest in greys, % in the centre
- Breakdown table: value, % and a comparison column (e.g. % of fleet)
- The key row highlighted

## Size
512×440. Only sizes from the layout grid.

## Style rules
- Follow the tokens and the quality bar in README.md.
- Title 19 px bold, hero 30 px bold, labels 13 px, axis 11 px. Tabular numbers.
- One accent highlight only. Red only for bad values, green only for on track.
- No empty space: if it doesn't fill the card, use the smaller size.
