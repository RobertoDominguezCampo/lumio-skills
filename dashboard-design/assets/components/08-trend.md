# Trend comparison

**Question it answers:** Are we ahead of last year, and since when?

## When to use it
Showing a change over time against a baseline.

## When not to
Under 6 points in time: use 01.

## Anatomy
- Insight title with the gap in accent
- Metric switch top right
- Hero total + change pill
- This year in accent, last year in grey, the gap shaded
- A marker and dark label where the story changes
- Line labels at the end, no legend

## Size
1024×440. Only sizes from the layout grid.

## Style rules
- Follow the tokens and the quality bar in README.md.
- Title 19 px bold, hero 30 px bold, labels 13 px, axis 11 px. Tabular numbers.
- One accent highlight only. Red only for bad values, green only for on track.
- No empty space: if it doesn't fill the card, use the smaller size.

## In Power BI
Power BI: line chart with two series, error band or area for the gap, reference line for the marker.
