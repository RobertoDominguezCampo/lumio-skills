# Variance bars

**Question it answers:** What grew and what fell vs last year?

## When to use it
Comparing a change across 5 to 12 members (stores, regions).

## When not to
Members don't share the same baseline: use % change, not absolute.

## Anatomy
- Insight title naming the outlier
- Hero total change top right with pill
- Diverging bars from a zero line, sorted
- Neutral bars for normal values, red for the bad one, its label in red
- One-line annotation that explains the outlier

## Size
1024×440. Only sizes from the layout grid.

## Style rules
- Follow the tokens and the quality bar in README.md.
- Title 19 px bold, hero 30 px bold, labels 13 px, axis 11 px. Tabular numbers.
- One accent highlight only. Red only for bad values, green only for on track.
- No empty space: if it doesn't fill the card, use the smaller size.
