# Ranking

**Question it answers:** Who are the top 10?

## When to use it
Long lists where a few members cause most of the result.

## When not to
Fewer than 5 members: use 02 or 04.

## Anatomy
- Insight title with the concentration %
- Top 10 bars with rank number and value
- The group that matters in accent with a bracket and its %
- The rest grouped as Others, below a dashed line

## Size
512×440. Only sizes from the layout grid.

## Style rules
- Follow the tokens and the quality bar in README.md.
- Title 19 px bold, hero 30 px bold, labels 13 px, axis 11 px. Tabular numbers.
- One accent highlight only. Red only for bad values, green only for on track.
- No empty space: if it doesn't fill the card, use the smaller size.
