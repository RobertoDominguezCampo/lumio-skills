# References

Visual references for the dashboard-design skill. Use them to calibrate quality, never to copy content.

## How to use them
- **fixed-reference.png** is the quality bar: the finish every prototype must reach (spacing, type scale, hierarchy, one accent colour, insight titles, closed components).
- **layouts/** holds real dashboards for some layout patterns. After you choose a layout, open its folder if there is one and check the proportions. Executive Z examples are in pro/ (files starting with ez-).
- **pro/** holds some of the best-scored pro dashboards from a review of 1,081 reports. Look at them before building components: how a KPI carries its context, how much white space, how few colours.
- Take patterns, not pixels. Never copy text, data, brands or logos.
- Images are small (about 500 px). Judge structure and hierarchy, not fine detail.

Every image here is a small thumbnail of a dashboard published on Tableau Public, credited and linked to its author below. They belong to their authors and are included for reference only. `fixed-reference.png` is Lumio Visuals' own work.
